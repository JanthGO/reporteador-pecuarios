# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Media analysts at Pecuarios (livestock/agricultural media platform) who monitor web traffic, visitor demographics, campaign performance, and content metrics across three divisions: Porcicultura, Ganadería, and Avicultura. They work in an office environment, reviewing dashboards daily to make data-driven editorial and business decisions.

## Product Purpose

A reporting dashboard that consolidates web analytics, visitor profiles, banner stats, email campaigns (Mailchimp), and content performance (articles, videos, events, products) into a single interface. Success means analysts can quickly identify trends, compare metrics across divisions, and export findings — without hunting through multiple tools.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a reliable instrument: precise, uncluttered, and authoritative. Confidence comes from clean data presentation, not visual flourish.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered dashboards with tiny fonts and overwhelming data dumps (data overload tools)
- Generic SaaS dashboards with cream backgrounds, gradient text, glassmorphism, and decorative AI aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — data speaks, UI steps back. Every visual element must serve comprehension.
2. **Information hierarchy** — the most important metric is always the most visible. Guide the eye, don't compete for it.
3. **Trust through consistency** — predictable patterns, reliable feedback, no surprises in behavior or appearance.
4. **Dense but readable** — maximize data density without overwhelming. Spacing and typography do the heavy lifting.
5. **Earned presence** — every element justifies its space. If it doesn't help the analyst work faster, it doesn't belong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Good color contrast (4.5:1 minimum for body text). Keyboard navigation throughout. Reduced motion alternatives for all animations. Considerations for color-blind users in data visualizations (patterns + color, not color alone).
